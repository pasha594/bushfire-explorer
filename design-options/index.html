bushfiremap.com · Design review

Ten directions for Bushfire Explorer

Each option is a working homepage built on the same content as the live site: the map with its overlays, warning counts, the ranked fire list, current warnings and the state list. All figures are real, taken from a snapshot of the agency feeds at 10:44 am AEDT on Tuesday 10 November 2026.

Six options keep today’s layout and change the type, colour and components. Four rearrange the page. Five are restrained enough to ship as they are; five are deliberately bolder. Each is shown in one colour mode; the one you choose gets its second mode next.

How to review

  • Open an option, then use the bar at the bottom left to step through the rest with ‹ and ›.
  • These work: the map’s Hotspots / Fire danger switch, paging the fire list, “Show all warnings”, the Atlas gazetteer and the Teletext page keys.
  • Search boxes, filters and links are visual only.
  • Maps are drawn from embedded outlines rather than map tiles, so you can judge how each option styles the map. The production basemap each would use is noted below.

Notes

What “not AI-generated” meant here. The competitor sites share a look: near-black ground, Inter-style type, glowing orange circles, rounded floating panels, emoji and pill badges. Every option here instead takes its form from a real Australian or public-information source: warning signage, topographic sheets, Bureau of Meteorology products, park signs, fire-appliance livery, print, atlases, transport boards and broadcast teletext. None uses Inter, glow effects or emoji.

Not yet designed. State pages, the fire detail page, the sources dialog and the second colour mode. These follow once a direction is chosen.

Data. 186 incidents from the seven state and territory feeds (93 active, 28 under control, 65 planned burns), 13 Advice warnings, 12,018 satellite hotspots (maps show a 3,200-point sample) and today’s fire danger ratings for 139 districts. Collected by running bushfiremap.com’s own feed code.

Files. Everything is in design-options/ on the claude/gallant-fermi-yu5hwi branch. Each option is one HTML file using shared data and map helpers in design-options/shared/.
